Lost your API key?

For your security, API keys (they start with mfk_) are shown once on the post-checkout claim page; only the SHA-256 hash is stored afterwards, so we cannot look a key up, but we can replace it. Private requests: email harbor-ops@mail.grokbot.com.

Check usage & cancel anytime

See your current billing period and remaining quota at any time with GET /v1/keys/me:

Check usage
curl -sS https://mentionforge.mentionforge.workers.dev/v1/keys/me \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY'

Cancel anytime or update your payment method in the Stripe Billing Portal by calling POST /v1/keys/portal with your API key:

Open billing portal
curl -sS -X POST https://mentionforge.mentionforge.workers.dev/v1/keys/portal \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY'

Open the returned url in your browser to manage or cancel your subscription. If you no longer have your API key or cannot reach the billing portal, contact support below with your checkout email and we will cancel it for you.

Cancellations & refunds

Cancelled subscriptions stop renewing and stay active until the end of the current billing period (period_end). Refund requests are handled case by case via support.
