About MentionForge

Cited social listening and brand research for AI agents and the people who build them, delivered over REST and the Model Context Protocol.

What MentionForge does

Ask about a brand, product, competitor, or topic and MentionForge returns one structured, cited brief: how much it is being discussed, the sentiment mix, recurring themes, and the individual mentions, each linked to its source.

It runs on Cloudflare Workers and offers two ways in: a REST API (POST /v1/research plus focused endpoints for comparisons, digests, risk, replies, mention exports, and trends) and a Streamable HTTP Model Context Protocol server at /mcp whose main tool is research_mentions.

Who it’s for

Agent builders

Give your agent one dependable tool for brand, product, and competitor questions instead of stitching together raw web searches.

Developers

Call one REST endpoint and get structured JSON with citations, request IDs, and retries that never charge twice.

Founders

See how your product and your competitors are discussed across news, the web, and review sites.

Marketers

Get share of voice, recurring themes, and early warning of negative spikes, with every mention linked to its source.

Where the data comes from

MentionForge searches public sources only:

  • News: Google News RSS, GDELT, and Hacker News.
  • Web: Wikipedia, Wikidata, and Brave Search when configured, plus GitHub Issues and Stack Overflow for developer topics.
  • Review sites: Trustpilot, G2, and Capterra, found through web search.

Native Reddit and X feeds are optional upgrades that need official API access, and they are off by default. Which sources are live right now is always published at GET /health.

How pricing and trials work

Discovery and grounding endpoints are free. Paid research calls cost $0.02 USDC per completed call on Base via the x402 payment standard, with 10 free trial calls per wallet via the X-Wallet header. Denied requests (such as rate-limit or quota errors) do not settle and are not charged. See pricing for worked examples.
